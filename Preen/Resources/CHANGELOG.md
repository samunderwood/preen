## 1.0.1

### Fixed
- Links unwrapped from Google, Outlook and other redirects keep line breaks and invisible characters escaped, so pasting one into Terminal can't run a command.

## 1.0

### New
- Preen takes tracking parameters and redirect wrappers out of links as you copy them, and leaves the rest of what you copied as it was.
- The menu bar shows what was removed from each link, with a way to restore the original.
- Leave particular sites or apps alone, or pause Preen for a while.
- Clean links in selected text from the Services menu, or in any text with the Clean Links action in Shortcuts.
